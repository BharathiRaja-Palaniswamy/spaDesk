# Family Spa — Business Overview

## About Family Spa

Family Spa is a neighborhood wellness spa founded in 2016 in Sunnyvale, California. The spa was created to make self-care approachable for individuals, couples, and families looking for a calm and welcoming place to recharge. Over time, the business grew from a small facial studio into a full-service wellness destination offering facials, massage therapy, body treatments, waxing, curated seasonal packages, and selected kid-friendly services.

The spa’s brand promise is simple: thoughtful care, transparent pricing, clean service standards, and a warm environment for both first-time and returning guests. Family Spa aims to balance a premium feel with accessible service options and a family-friendly atmosphere.

## Mission

To help customers feel refreshed, confident, and cared for through personalized wellness and skincare experiences delivered in a calm, trustworthy, and family-friendly setting.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you offer services for children?**  
Yes. Family Spa offers selected kid-friendly services designed to be gentle, short, and age-appropriate.

**Do I need an appointment?**  
Walk-ins are welcome when availability permits, but appointments are strongly recommended, especially for evenings and weekends.

**How early should I arrive?**  
Customers are encouraged to arrive 10 minutes early for check-in and consultation.

**Do you offer gift cards?**  
Yes. Digital and physical gift cards are available.

**Can I request a specific therapist or esthetician?**  
Yes, requests can be made during booking, though availability is not guaranteed.

**What should I do if I have sensitive skin?**  
Customers should mention skin sensitivities during booking or before treatment so staff can guide them to appropriate services.

**Do you provide medical skincare advice?**  
No. Services are wellness and cosmetic in nature and do not replace medical or dermatological care.

**Can services be customized?**  
Some services may be adjusted based on customer preferences and staff guidance.

## Policies

### Booking Policy
- Appointments may be booked online, by phone, or in person.
- A valid phone number and name are required to reserve an appointment.
- Some premium or package bookings may require a card on file.

### Cancellation Policy
- Cancellations or rescheduling requests should be made at least 24 hours in advance.
- Late cancellations may incur a 50% service fee.
- No-shows may be charged 100% of the reserved service cost.

### Late Arrival Policy
- If a customer arrives late, the session may be shortened to avoid delaying the next guest.
- Full service charges may still apply depending on the delay.

### Refund Policy
- Services already completed are non-refundable.
- Retail product returns are accepted within 14 days if unopened and in original condition.

### Health and Safety Policy
- Customers should share allergies, sensitivities, pregnancy status, or recent procedures before the service begins.
- The spa reserves the right to decline or modify a service for safety reasons.
- The spa does not diagnose medical conditions.

### Age Policy
- Customers under 18 must have a parent or guardian consent for selected services.
- Kid-friendly services are intended for children ages 6 to 12 unless otherwise approved by staff.
