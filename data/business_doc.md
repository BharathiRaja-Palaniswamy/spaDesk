# Pebble Spa — Business Overview

## About

Pebble Spa is a neighborhood wellness spa founded in 2016 in Sunnyvale, California. The spa was created to make self-care approachable for individuals, couples, and families looking for a calm and welcoming place to recharge. Over time, the business grew from a small facial studio into a full-service wellness destination offering facials, massage therapy, body treatments, waxing, curated seasonal packages, and selected kid-friendly services.

The spa’s brand promise is simple: thoughtful care, transparent pricing, clean service standards, and a warm environment for both first-time and returning guests. Pebble Spa aims to balance a premium feel with accessible service options and a family-friendly atmosphere.

In 2024 Pebble Spa opened the Pebble Studio, a light-filled yoga and movement room on the second floor. The studio offers small group classes, private sessions, and guided meditation, so guests can pair a treatment with a class or build a weekly practice.

## Mission

To help customers feel refreshed, confident, and cared for through personalized wellness and skincare experiences delivered in a calm, trustworthy, and family-friendly setting.

## Service Suitability

### Pregnancy
- Prenatal Massage and Prenatal Yoga are designed for pregnancy and are offered after the first trimester.
- Heat-based treatments are not offered during pregnancy.
- Deep Tissue Massage and the Detox Body Scrub are not offered during pregnancy.
- Facials are available with pregnancy-safe products. Please tell your esthetician before the service.

### Heat-Based Treatments
- The Sea Mineral Body Wrap and the hot stone enhancement use heat.
- Heat-based treatments are not recommended for guests with high blood pressure, heart conditions, or diabetes without clearance from a healthcare provider.

### Skin Sensitivity and Recent Procedures
- The Deep Renewal Facial and the Brightening Vitamin C Facial are not suitable within 7 days of a chemical peel, laser treatment, or sunburn.
- Waxing is not offered on skin treated with retinoids in the past 7 days.
- Guests with rosacea or reactive skin are guided to the Calming Sensitive Skin Facial.

### Movement and Health Conditions
- Vinyasa Flow includes inversions. Guests with high blood pressure, glaucoma, or a recent neck injury should tell the instructor, who will offer alternatives.
- Restorative Yoga and Guided Meditation and Breathwork suit most guests, including those recovering from injury.
- Deep Tissue Massage is not recommended for guests taking blood thinners or with a recent injury without clearance from a healthcare provider.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you offer services for children?**  
Yes. Pebble Spa offers selected kid-friendly services designed to be gentle, short, and age-appropriate.

**Do I need an appointment?**  
Walk-ins are welcome when availability permits, but appointments are strongly recommended, especially for evenings and weekends.

**How early should I arrive?**  
Customers are encouraged to arrive 10 minutes early for check-in and consultation.

**Do you offer gift cards?**  
Yes. Digital and physical gift cards are available.

**Can I request a specific therapist or esthetician?**  
Yes, requests can be made during booking, though availability is not guaranteed.

**What should I do if I have sensitive skin?**  
Customers should mention skin sensitivities during booking or before treatment so staff can guide them to appropriate services.

**Do you provide medical skincare advice?**  
No. Services are wellness and cosmetic in nature and do not replace medical or dermatological care.

**Can services be customized?**  
Some services may be adjusted based on customer preferences and staff guidance.

**Do I need yoga experience to join a class?**  
No. Sunrise Hatha Flow, Restorative Yoga, and Guided Meditation and Breathwork welcome complete beginners. Vinyasa Flow is best after a few classes.

**Do I need to bring a yoga mat?**  
No. Mats and props are provided. You are welcome to bring your own.

**Can I combine a class with a spa treatment?**  
Yes. The Stretch & Soothe Package pairs a Restorative Yoga class with an Aromatherapy Massage, and any class can be booked before or after a treatment.

**Is there parking?**  
Yes. There is a free parking lot behind the building, including accessible spaces.

## Policies

### Booking Policy
- Appointments may be booked online, by phone, or in person.
- A valid phone number and name are required to reserve an appointment.
- Some premium or package bookings may require a card on file.

### Cancellation Policy
- Cancellations or rescheduling requests should be made at least 24 hours in advance.
- Late cancellations may incur a 50% service fee.
- No-shows may be charged 100% of the reserved service cost.

### Class Booking Policy
- Group classes can be booked up to 14 days ahead.
- Cancel at least 12 hours before class to keep your class credit or get a refund on a drop-in.
- Late cancellations and no-shows use one class from your pass, or forfeit the drop-in price.
- When a class is full, guests can join the waitlist and are notified if a spot opens.

### Late Arrival Policy
- If a customer arrives late, the session may be shortened to avoid delaying the next guest.
- Full service charges may still apply depending on the delay.

### Refund Policy
- Services already completed are non-refundable.
- Retail product returns are accepted within 14 days if unopened and in original condition.

### Health and Safety Policy
- Customers should share allergies, sensitivities, pregnancy status, or recent procedures before the service begins.
- The spa reserves the right to decline or modify a service for safety reasons.
- The spa does not diagnose medical conditions.

### Age Policy
- Customers under 18 must have a parent or guardian consent for selected services.
- Kid-friendly services are intended for children ages 6 to 12 unless otherwise approved by staff.
- Adult yoga classes are open to guests 16 and older. Guests aged 16 and 17 need a parent or guardian’s consent.
